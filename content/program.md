# What we do

**Edmonton Go Tech Meetup** is built around solving problems with open technology. Go is our go-to language for backend services, CLIs and tooling, but the subject of any given night is a real engineering or business problem — and the open-source tools that solve it.

## Recurring formats

### 🐹 Go Night

Talks and lightning talks with Go front and centre: backend services, concurrency, CLIs, tooling, performance, and what a problem looks like when solved in Go. Typically three 15-minute talks rather than one long presentation.

### 🧱 Open Source Stack Night

A deep dive into one open technology and the problem it solves. Topics we'd like to cover include PostgreSQL, Incus/LXC, ZFS, Caddy, Grafana/Prometheus, MinIO/S3 and networking.

### 🔨 Build & Hack Night

No slides. Bring a laptop, a project, or a problem you're stuck on, and work on it with other people.

### 🧑‍💻 Workshop

Hands-on, guided and practical — everyone leaves with something working. Example: *Build a Go API with PostgreSQL*.

### 🏠 Show Me Your Homelab

A show-and-tell for self-hosters and infrastructure enthusiasts: five 10-minute tours of home labs, servers, storage and networks.

## Tracks

Rather than separating by language, we organize around the problems:

- **Go** — services, APIs, CLI, concurrency, tooling
- **Data** — PostgreSQL, SQL, object storage, backups
- **Systems** — Linux, containers, virtualization, ZFS
- **Infrastructure** — networking, DevOps, observability, security

Everything is tied together by **open source and practical engineering**.
