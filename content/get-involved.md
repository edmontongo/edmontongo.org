# Get involved

Edmonton Go Tech Meetup is community-run, and there are several easy ways to take part. You don't need to be an expert — you just need to be curious.

## Give a talk

Short talks are welcome and encouraged. A lightning talk is **15 minutes**, and you can talk about:

- a problem you solved at work
- a Go feature, library or pattern
- an open-source tool you use (PostgreSQL, Incus, ZFS, Grafana…)
- something you built, broke, or are still figuring out

Reach out with a topic and a rough title, and we'll find you a slot.

## Suggest a topic

Want a night on PostgreSQL performance, containers, storage or something else? Tell us. The program is shaped by what people want to learn.

## Host or sponsor

We meet at Edmonton Unlimited. If your company would like to host a night or sponsor pizza, get in touch — no sales pitches required.

## Organize

A meetup runs best with more than one organizer. If you'd like to help keep things consistent, we'd love the help.

## Come along

The simplest way to get involved is to show up. Beginners, career-changers, students, homelabbers and seasoned engineers are all welcome.

Find the next meetup on the [Meetups](/meetup/) page.
