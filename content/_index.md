# Edmonton Go Tech Meetup

{{< figure src="/images/Go-Logo_Blue.png" alt="Go Logo" width="150px" height="150px" >}}

**Go, backend & open-source infrastructure.** We're a community for Edmonton developers, sysadmins, homelabbers and tech enthusiasts who build and run software with open technologies.

Go is our go-to language — for backend services, CLIs, tooling and systems work. But the problems we face are more consistent than the languages we use to solve them, so we organize around the problems and use an open-source mindset to solve them together.

## What to expect

- **Go Nights** — talks and lightning talks, Go front and centre
- **Open Source Stack Nights** — one open technology, deep dive
- **Build & Hack Nights** — bring a project or a problem
- **Workshops** — hands-on; leave with something working
- **Show Me Your Homelab** — show-and-tell for self-hosters

You don't need to know Go to join us. If you're curious about backend, infrastructure or open source, you're welcome. No sales pitches — just people solving problems. [See the full program →](/program/)
